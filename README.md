# 🎵 Spotify Streaming History Dashboard — Power BI

Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Spotify streaming history from 2013–2024 to uncover listening behavior, artist popularity, music discovery trends, and user engagement patterns.

---

##  Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Spotify streaming history data to understand user listening behavior, artist engagement, streaming patterns, and music discovery trends over time.

This dashboard helps uncover insights into user engagement habits and long-term streaming behavior.

---

## 📊 Business Questions Answered

1. Which artists received the highest listening time between 2013 and 2024?
2. What time of day do users stream music the most?
3. Which songs have the highest play duration?
4. How does music listening behavior vary across different years?
5. Which tracks are skipped the most and which are completed more often?
6. What is the ratio of new music discovery across different months?
7. Which months recorded the highest engagement with new music?
8. What trends can be identified in user streaming habits over time?
9. Which artists maintain consistent listener engagement across multiple years?
10. How do listening patterns differ between morning, afternoon, evening, and night?

---

##  Key Insights

###  Night-Time Streaming Dominates User Activity

Night-time listening contributes the largest share of streaming activity, accounting for nearly half of total listening time.

**Business Value**
- Spotify can prioritize personalized nighttime playlists.
- Advertisers can target users during peak engagement periods.
- Artists releasing relaxing or late-night music may gain higher engagement.

---

###  Classic Rock Artists Drive High Engagement

Artists such as The Beatles, The Killers, and John Mayer generated the highest streaming duration.

**Business Value**
- Classic and alternative rock genres maintain strong listener loyalty.
- Spotify can continue investing in nostalgia-based playlists.
- Record labels can leverage catalog music for long-term engagement.

---

###  Certain Tracks Experience High Skip Rates

Some tracks recorded high play counts but were frequently skipped.

**Business Value**
- High skip rates may indicate weaker listener retention.
- Recommendation systems can be improved using skip behavior analysis.
- Artists can optimize track structure to improve engagement.

---

### 🎶 Music Discovery Peaks During Specific Months

September and October recorded the highest ratio of new music discovery.

**Business Value**
- Users appear more willing to explore new music during these periods.
- Spotify can schedule campaigns and releases strategically.
- Marketing teams can maximize seasonal engagement opportunities.

---

### 📈 Long-Term Streaming Trends Show Consistent Engagement

Streaming activity remained consistent from 2013–2024, showing sustained platform engagement over time.

**Business Value**
- Strong long-term user retention is evident.
- Historical streaming behavior can support forecasting.
- Subscription retention strategies appear effective.

---

##  Recommendations

- Promote personalized playlists during peak night-time listening hours.
- Invest in recommendation systems that reduce high-skip tracks.
- Launch new music campaigns during high-discovery months.
- Continue leveraging nostalgia and classic rock playlists for engagement.
- Use historical streaming behavior to improve personalization strategies.
