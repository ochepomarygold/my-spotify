## Project Overview
Developed an interactive Spotify Streaming History Dashboard in Microsoft Power BI to analyze user listening behavior from 2013–2024. The dashboard provides insights into artist popularity, streaming patterns, music discovery trends, and user engagement across different time periods.

---

## Business Questions Answered by the Dashboard
1 Which artists received the highest listening time between 2013 and 2024?
2 What time of day do users stream music the most?
3 Which songs have the highest play duration?
4 How does music listening behavior vary across different years?
5 Which tracks are skipped the most and which are completed more often?
6 What is the ratio of new music discovery across different months?
7 Which months recorded the highest engagement with new music?
8 What trends can be identified in user streaming habits over time?
9 Which artists maintain consistent listener engagement across multiple years?
10 How do listening patterns differ between morning, afternoon, evening, and night?

---

## Key Business Insights
#### Night-Time Streaming Dominates User Activity

The dashboard shows that the Night time period contributes the largest share of total streaming activity, accounting for nearly half of all listening time.

###### Business Value:
Spotify can prioritize personalized nighttime playlists and recommendations.
Advertisers can target users more effectively during peak engagement hours.
Artists releasing relaxing or late-night themed music may gain higher engagement.

##### 2. Classic Rock Artists Drive High Engagement

###### Artists such as:

The Beatles
The Killers
John Mayer
generated the highest streaming duration.

###### Business Value:
Classic and alternative rock genres maintain strong long-term listener loyalty.
Spotify can continue investing in nostalgia-based playlist curation.
Record labels can leverage catalog music for sustained revenue generation.
##### 3. Certain Tracks Experience High Skip Rates

Some songs recorded high play counts but were also frequently skipped.

###### Business Value:
High skip rates may indicate weak listener retention or playlist mismatch.
Spotify can improve recommendation algorithms by reducing repetitive or low-retention tracks.
Artists can analyze track structure and intro duration to improve engagement.
##### 4. Music Discovery Peaks During Specific Months

Months such as September and October showed the highest “Ratio of New Music.”

###### Business Value:
Users are more willing to explore new content during these periods.
Spotify can schedule album launches, campaigns, and recommendation pushes strategically.
Marketing teams can maximize engagement with seasonal discovery campaigns.
##### 5. Long-Term Streaming Trends Show Consistent User Engagement

The yearly filter demonstrates continued listening activity from 2013–2024, showing sustained platform engagement over time.

###### Business Value:
Spotify benefits from strong long-term user retention.
Historical streaming data can help forecast future listening behavior.
Subscription retention strategies appear effective.
